To My Skeleton — My GEM 💙

A little something I wanted to write for you...

Skelettooonnn 😂

Happy Birthdayyy! 🎂

Honestly, I don't know how to start,when I think about how we became this close, it actually feels a little funny. In first year, we didn't even have much bonding. We just knew each other.

And then somehow, especially in second year second semester, we became really close. I think I should thank Sharanya. We met and became close because of her. 😂

But after that, you slowly became someone really special to me.

You are not just one of my college friends anymore. You became someone who understands me, listens to me, talks to me properly and genuinely cares about me.You were there whenever I needed someone to talk to. Even the small things,like just checking on me..they may feel small for you..but very special for me.

"Some people become important to us without us even realizing when it happened." 💙

And one thing I will always remember is how you stayed there for me, especially during internship. When many people were busy with their own things or were not around, you were still there.You didn't left me without clarifying.

Maybe it sounds like a small thing, but for me it isn't. Having someone who is constantly there and whom you can actually count on means a lot.

Somewhere along the way, you became my non-blood brother. 🤍

And I don't think you know how special that is for me.

I'm a single child, and I never had a brother. So when I tied Rakhi to you, you were the first person outside my family to whom I had ever tied one.

That may look like a simple thing to someone else, but it is actually very very special to me.

You are the GEM — the brother I got in this college. 💙

And obviously, I cannot write a letter to you without mentioning our legendary Motu and Patlu combination. 😂Yesss. You are tall and I am short. The way you call me "CATUU" I really like it.

Jokes apart, I'm really grateful that I met you.

College gave me many people, but only a few actually become a part of my life in a meaningful way.

You are one of those people for me.

We still have more college life left, so I hope we make many more stupid memories, laugh over the most random things, annoy each other, tease each other and somehow survive these remaining years together. 😂

And even after college, I hope this bond stays the same, no matter where life takes us.

Not brothers by blood,
but definitely brother by bond. 💙

So on your birthday, I just want to say thank you.

Thank you for being there.
And thank you for becoming the brother I never had.

I hope you achieve everything you want, stay happy, stay healthy and keep being the same person you are.

And yes, Skeleton... please don't become any taller. 😂 Standing beside you is already enough height difference for me.

Once again, Happy Birthday, Sasimanth! 🎂💙

With lots of love 💙
Saniya(Catuu),
